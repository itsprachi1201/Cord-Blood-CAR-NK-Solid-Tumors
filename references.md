# References

1. Sarvaria A, Jawdat D, Madrigal JA, Saudemont A. Umbilical Cord Blood Natural Killer Cells, Their Characteristics, and Potential Clinical Applications. *Frontiers in Immunology*. 2017. — Primary source for cord blood NK cell immaturity phenotype (decreased CD16, DNAM-1, NKG2C, granzyme B, perforin; increased NKG2A), and for IL-15/IL-2 restoring cytotoxicity.

2. Lee J, Song J, Jung D, Choi E, Jo SG, Kim S. Therapeutic potential of anti-ErbB3 chimeric antigen receptor natural killer cells against breast cancer. *Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy*. 2025. — Source for CAR-NK engineering against breast cancer, IL-15 secreting constructs, tumor infiltration data, and discussion of open challenges (off-target risk, KIR genotyping gaps, ErbB3/TIL relationship).

3. Giraldo NA, Sanchez-Salas R, Peske JD, Vano Y, Becht E, Petitprez F, et al. The clinical role of the TME in solid cancer. *British Journal of Cancer*. 2019. — Direct source for tumor microenvironment immunosuppression mechanisms (TGF-beta, checkpoint signaling, and related pathways) referenced in the compounding bottleneck discussion. Cited within Kim et al. 2025's own reference list, confirming its relevance to this exact research area.
