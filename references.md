# References

1. Sarvaria A, Jawdat D, Madrigal JA, Saudemont A. Umbilical Cord Blood Natural Killer Cells, Their Characteristics, and Potential Clinical Applications. *Frontiers in Immunology*. 2017. — Primary source for cord blood NK cell immaturity phenotype (decreased CD16, DNAM-1, NKG2C, granzyme B, perforin; increased NKG2A), and for IL-15/IL-2 restoring cytotoxicity.

2. Lee J, Song J, Jung D, Choi E, Jo SG, Kim S. Therapeutic potential of anti-ErbB3 chimeric antigen receptor natural killer cells against breast cancer. *Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy*. 2025. — Source for CAR-NK engineering against breast cancer, IL-15 secreting constructs, tumor infiltration data, and discussion of open challenges (off-target risk, KIR genotyping gaps, ErbB3/TIL relationship).

3. Giraldo NA, Sanchez-Salas R, Peske JD, Vano Y, Becht E, Petitprez F, et al. The clinical role of the TME in solid cancer. *British Journal of Cancer*. 2019. — Direct source for tumor microenvironment immunosuppression mechanisms (TGF-beta, checkpoint signaling, and related pathways) referenced in the compounding bottleneck discussion. Cited within Lee et al. 2025's own reference list, confirming its relevance to this exact research area.

4. Wu F, Zhang P, Wu W, Hou T, Jiang X, Huang T. Research progress on tumor extracellular matrix stiffness and immunosuppression. *Frontiers in Immunology*. 2026;17:1852616. doi: 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1852616. — Source for the physical ECM barrier layer: LOX-driven collagen cross-linking stiffens the tumor extracellular matrix, restricting NK cell infiltration depth independent of biochemical suppression (Section 2.2.4, "NK cell"); also source for MMP/enzymatic degradation and LOX inhibition as countermeasures (Section 4.1).
