# References

1. Sarvaria A, Jawdat D, Madrigal JA, Saudemont A. Umbilical Cord Blood Natural Killer Cells, Their Characteristics, and Potential Clinical Applications. *Frontiers in Immunology*. 2017. — Primary source for cord blood NK cell immaturity phenotype (decreased CD16, DNAM-1, NKG2C, granzyme B, perforin; increased NKG2A), and for IL-15/IL-2 restoring cytotoxicity.

2. Lee J, Song J, Yoo W, Choi H, Jung D, Choi E, Jo SG, Gong EY, Jeoung YH, Park YS, Son WC, Lee H, Lee H, Kim JJ, Kim T, Lee S, Park JJ, Kim TD, Kim S. Therapeutic potential of anti-ErbB3 chimeric antigen receptor natural killer cells against breast cancer. *Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy*. 2025;74(2):73. doi: 10.1007/s00262-024-03923-y. — Source for CAR-NK engineering against breast cancer using cord blood mononuclear cells, IL-15 secreting constructs, tumor infiltration data, and discussion of open challenges (off-target risk, KIR genotyping gaps, ErbB3/TIL relationship). Kim S (senior/corresponding author) is Professor Kim Seok-ho, Dong-A University.

3. Giraldo NA, Sanchez-Salas R, Peske JD, Vano Y, Becht E, Petitprez F, et al. The clinical role of the TME in solid cancer. *British Journal of Cancer*. 2019. — Direct source for tumor microenvironment immunosuppression mechanisms (TGF-beta, checkpoint signaling, and related pathways) referenced in the compounding bottleneck discussion.

4. Wu F, Zhang P, Wu W, Hou T, Jiang X, Huang T. Research progress on tumor extracellular matrix stiffness and immunosuppression. *Frontiers in Immunology*. 2026;17:1852616. doi: 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1852616. — Source for the physical barrier layer. Important nuance: this review shows ECM stiffness does NOT act independently of biochemical signaling — it directly suppresses NK cell cytotoxicity through mechanotransduction pathways (YAP/TAZ, Piezo1; Section 2.2.4), and indirectly activates cancer-associated fibroblasts that upregulate PD-L1 (Section 3), coupling the physical and biochemical layers rather than keeping them separate. Also source for MMP/enzymatic degradation and LOX inhibition as countermeasures.

### Note on citation practice
Reference 3 (Giraldo et al.) is cited here for the general TME immunosuppression mechanisms it describes; it was located independently via search rather than read via Lee et al.'s bibliography. Only claims this review actually makes are attributed to it.
